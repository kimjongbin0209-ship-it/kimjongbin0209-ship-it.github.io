About

I am an undergraduate student at KAIST pursuing a double major in electrical engineering and computer science. My research interests focus on foundational machine learning, world models, and reinforcement learning.

I am drawn to research that questions the assumptions behind how models learn and reason. My interests include new learning principles and architectures, the role of memory in reasoning, and representations that capture compositional structure. I value mathematical elegance and explanatory power, and hope to study ideas that open new ways of thinking about learning and intelligence.

Current Research Interests

My current questions include whether world models can learn reusable rules, how memory supports compositional reasoning, and when better prediction leads to better planning. 3D Gaussian Splatting

Scene Reconstruction & Novel-view Synthesis with gsplat

Built a pure-PyTorch renderer and a training pipeline on gsplat's CUDA rasterizer for COLMAP-based 3D scene reconstruction. Trained on 135 registered views for 30,000 steps, achieving a validation PSNR of 27.354 dB and L1 error of 0.03535.

StarGAN v2

Multi-Domain Image Translation on AFHQ

Implemented a PyTorch framework for 256 × 256 image translation across cat, dog, and wildlife domains using AdaIN-based style conditioning. Trained with PyTorch MPS on an Apple M4 Pro and evaluated a 20,000-step checkpoint on 3,000 generated images, recording an overall FID of 44.11 and LPIPS diversity of 0.4270.
