About

I am an undergraduate student at KAIST studying electrical engineering and computer science-related topics. My primary research interest is reinforcement learning, particularly learning systems that acquire useful representations and decision-making capabilities through interaction rather than heavy supervision.

I am also interested in world models, LLM post-training, multimodal foundation models, and efficient generative models. Ultimately, I hope to study learning principles that enable increasingly autonomous and general agents.
