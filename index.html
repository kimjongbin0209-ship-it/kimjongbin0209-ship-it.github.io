About

I am an undergraduate student at KAIST pursuing a double major in electrical engineering and computer science. My interests span foundational machine learning, world models and reinforcement learning, learning theory, theoretical computer science, and mathematics.

I am drawn to research that combines mathematical elegance with conceptual insight. I enjoy working with precise definitions and proofs and understanding the connections between mathematical structures. 3D Gaussian Splatting

Scene Reconstruction & Novel-view Synthesis with gsplat

Built a pure-PyTorch renderer and a training pipeline on gsplat's CUDA rasterizer for COLMAP-based 3D scene reconstruction. Trained on 135 registered views for 30,000 steps, achieving a validation PSNR of 27.354 dB and L1 error of 0.03535.

StarGAN v2

Multi-Domain Image Translation on AFHQ

Implemented a PyTorch framework for 256 × 256 image translation across cat, dog, and wildlife domains using AdaIN-based style conditioning. Trained with PyTorch MPS on an Apple M4 Pro and evaluated a 20,000-step checkpoint on 3,000 generated images, recording an overall FID of 44.11 and LPIPS diversity of 0.4270.
